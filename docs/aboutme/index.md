# About Me

  My name is Bryson Bare, and I am a mechanical engineering student with a strong interest in problem-solving, designing, and understanding how mechanical components work. I chose mechanical engineering because I've always been interested in machines, tech, and the process of turning an idea into something that actually works. I've always enjoyed working through problems, learning new things, and finding ways to improve designs. I am especially interested in the hands-on process, like working with components.
  
   Mechanical engineering stood out to me because it combines many of my interests into one career. It allows me to use math, science, and problem-solving skills to solve real-world problems. I really enjoy the hands-on side of engineering as well as the design process. Being able to create, design, test, and identify problems and make changes to something I've created has always interested me. I also like mechanical engineering because it offers many different career paths and different opportunities to learn different things.
 
  As an engineering student, I am working hard every day to become a dependable, hardworking, and knowledgeable engineer. I want to keep building my skills in CAD, manufacturing, design, problem-solving, and teamwork. I am also learning that there is more to being an engineer than being able to solve problems, but they also require the skills to be able to defend any decison they make.
    
  My ultimate goal is to become an engineer who can take on challenging problems and contribute useful ideas to a team. As I gain more education and experience, I want to be more confident in my engineering decisions and develop the skills necessary to turn my ideas into effective real-world solutions.
