# About Me

  My name is Bryson Bare and I am a student of mechanical engineering who has a great deal of interest in problem-solving, in designing, and in understanding how mechanical parts function. I decided on a career in mechanical engineering since I have always been fascinated by machines, technology, and the way in which an idea can be turned into a working thing. I have always liked tackling problems, acquiring new knowledge, and looking for ways to improve designs. I am particularly interested in the practical aspect of the work, such as working with components.

What attracted me about mechanical engineering is that it brings together a number of things that I'm interested in into a single career. It gives me the opportunity to apply my knowledge of maths, science, and problem-solving to deal with real-world problems. I enjoy the practical aspect of engineering as much as I do the process of design. There has always been something that appealed to me about being able to create, design, test, identify problems, and then make changes to something I've made. I also like mechanical engineering since it provides a variety of career options and allows for the chance to learn a range of different things.

Since I have been studying engineering, I have been working every day to develop myself into a reliable, diligent and well-informed engineer. My aim is to continue improving my abilities in CAD, manufacturing, design, problem-solving and working as part of a team. At the same time, I am realizing that being an engineer involves more than just being able to solve problems, since it is also necessary to have the skills to defend any decisions you take.

My dream is to become an engineer capable of tackling difficult problems and offering valuable ideas to a team. The more education and experience I acquire, the more confident I want to become in my engineering decisions and the skills I want to develop in order to turn my ideas into practical real-world solutions.
