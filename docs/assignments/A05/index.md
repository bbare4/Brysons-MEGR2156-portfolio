#Assignment #5

For this assignment, the goal was to design a bracket. The bracket must slide smoothly over a T-beam and support a 600 lbf load. The bracket is to be designed with a safety factor of 4, assuming that the T-beam is rigid. I used aluminum 6061-T6 with a yield strength of 40,000 psi and a Young's modulus of 10,000,000 psi. Some approximations were made and will be noted throughout the work below. Assume no failure due to shear.
