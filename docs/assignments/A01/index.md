# A1 – [Build Your Professional Portfolio]
Website 1 - Portfolio Analysis

Navigability: This portfolio is easy to navigate because each assignment is on its own dedicated page. A reader can locate Assignment 1 without having to look through unrelated material. However, the assignment page itself covers several topics in long paragraphs, so finding specific portions of the analysis may take longer than if the content were split into clearly labeled sections.

Reproducibility: The portfolio provides useful information on the football's materials, geometry, components, and manufacturing process, but it does not include enough information for someone else to fully reproduce the product. For Example, it identifies that leather or a leather composite is used for the exterior, nylon laces, and a latex bladder, but it does not provide dimensions, tolerances, material specifications, or drawings needed for someone else to replicate it without questions. Therefore, a mechanical engineer could understand how the product functions and what it is made of, but without additional information, they cannot replicate it.
